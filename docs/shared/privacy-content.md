## Information We Collect

Aurora is provided by Talysson da Silva Oliveira, a sole trader trading as
Leangency ("Leangency", "we", "us"), the controller of the personal data
described in this policy. We collect account registration details, workspace
configuration data, product usage telemetry, and support communications that are
necessary to provide Aurora. We do not collect more information than is required
to operate, secure, and improve the service.

## How We Use Information

We use personal data to authenticate users, deliver CRM workflows, monitor
reliability, investigate abuse, respond to support requests, and meet legal and
contractual obligations. Where we rely on consent, customers can review linked
policy information before taking action.

## Sharing and Subprocessors

We share data only with service providers and subprocessors that help us host,
secure, monitor, and support Aurora. Every processor relationship is reviewed
for security, confidentiality, and data-governance obligations before production
use.

## Retention and Security

We retain data according to documented retention policies, customer agreements,
and regulatory requirements. Security controls include encryption in transit,
encryption at rest, role-based access control, audit logging, and
privacy-by-design reviews for new features.

## Your Rights

Depending on the applicable law, individuals can request access, correction,
export, objection, restriction, or deletion of eligible personal data. Requests
that affect regulated or contract-bound records may be fulfilled within the
limits of legal retention obligations.

## Contact Us

If you have privacy questions, data-subject requests, or concerns about this
policy, contact Leangency at privacy@leangency.com. We review policy changes
before release and publish an updated version identifier whenever the policy
changes materially.
